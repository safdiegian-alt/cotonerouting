# Operational rules — September 9, 2026

- The original NP identifier stays on every stop and export. Assignment groups are separate identifiers.
- If any delivery exists for an NP in the selected day, its pickups and deliveries remain on one vehicle with pickup-before-delivery. Explicit direct-transfer notes retain their existing grouping rule.
- Pickup-only NPs may be split across vehicles. Every pickup without a same-day delivery is depot-bound. A depot visit unloads only these pickups; same-day delivery goods remain aboard. If depot-bound goods remain after the last intermediate depot visit, final return and the configured Campana service time are included, even when optional final return is unchecked.
- Retiro, San Nicolás, Monserrat and San Telmo use GCBA barrio polygons. Microcentro is contained in them. Congreso is an operational rectangle: latitude -34.614 to -34.605, longitude -58.400 to -58.390. This deliberately includes the surroundings of the square rather than inventing an official Congreso barrio. Source: https://cdn.buenosaires.gob.ar/datosabiertos/datasets/barrios/barrios.geojson
- All groups with stops in this Center area merge into one exclusive assignment group for the whole day, not per trip. If the entire group cannot be scheduled, it is reported pending rather than split across two vehicles. Through-traffic remains under Google's route selection; this rule restricts servicing, not roads.
- Optional intermediate unloading is evaluated if two trips are enabled. It needs depot-bound cargo already collected. A corridor transition is an angular change over 60 degrees relative to Campana; detour via Campana must add at most 5 driving minutes compared with the direct leg. The configured depot service time is added separately and all windows/shift end remain mandatory. Useful unloading may increase km. Without this criterion, an insertion must improve the existing time/wait/km cost to be accepted. At most one intermediate depot stop is selected.
- Manual reassignment reorders affected routes with a bounded multi-start insertion search; it preserves center exclusivity, dependencies, stop coverage, shift hours and depot return accounting. A Center reassignment moves the complete Center group, as disclosed beside the button. Work runs in the solver worker. Failure keeps the previous plan; success clears cached road/map data and refreshes CSV/Maps/timelines.
- Readiness of independent pickup-only movements is assessed separately. A pending pickup does not unnecessarily block another independent pickup of the same NP. Delivery-linked groups retain all-or-none readiness.

Verification: operational-rules.mjs, manual-routing.mjs, territory-integration.mjs and import-and-vehicle-hours.mjs. Synthetic matrices test constraints, not live Google road distances. A new production calculation is needed for a like-for-like km comparison on the uploaded day, including actual saved address/hour corrections. No optimality guarantee is claimed.

## Reproducible search
Search phases use fixed work limits (256 partitions, 2048 exception trials, 30 reorder passes; fallback 5000 iterations and 4 polishing passes). The seeded PRNG is unchanged. Wall clock values are telemetry only; worker termination by timeout is removed, including manual reassignment. Equal ordered inputs/configuration/matrix produce equal routing output irrespective of CPU speed. Google traffic/matrix refreshes are external input changes, not covered by this invariance. Complete feasible territorial candidates are retained even when soft workload targets are exceeded, instead of silently discarding them and switching to fallback.
